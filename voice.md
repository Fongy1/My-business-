# voice.md

Built from: messages in this session + one reference email ("Just following up"). Small sample — update this as more real writing comes in.

## Tone (3-5 adjectives)

- **Direct** — says the thing without warming up to it. "I walked away from nothing" not "To answer your question about my background..."
- **Unpolished** — doesn't proofread for polish. Typos, phonetic spelling ("faulx", "belive", "uo and coming"), lowercase "i" mid-sentence — this is real, not to be cleaned up in casual writing.
- **Blunt-honest** — admits what he doesn't know instead of dressing it up. "I'm still unsure and trying to figure that out." "I'm figuring out my audience don't know who I'm trying to target yet."
- **Mate-warm** — casual Aussie warmth in anything sent to another person: "mate," "no stress," "reckon." Warmth comes from plain reassurance, not enthusiasm or exclamation points.
- **No-fluff** — gets to the point in one or two sentences. Doesn't pad.

## Word rules

**Never:**
- "At your earliest convenience"
- "Furthermore" / "Moreover"
- "With regard to the aforementioned..."
- "I am writing to formally..." / "formally enquire"
- "I wanted to reach out" (especially corporate/LinkedIn register)
- "Moving forward"
- "Touch base" / "Circle back"
- "Leverage"
- "Utilise" — use "use"
- "Delighted" — use "happy"
- "Dear Sir/Madam" / "Kind regards" / "I hope this email finds you well" — the whole formal-letter register is off-limits, not just individual words

**Always (when they fit naturally):**
- "mate"
- "reckon"
- "no stress"
- "give it a crack"
- "figuring out" / "still figuring out"
- "sort something out"

## Sentence structure

- Short, plain sentences. Rarely more than one comma-joined thought.
- Contractions always: "wasn't," "haven't," "I'm," "it's."
- No exclamation points.
- In chat/casual writing: capitalization and punctuation are optional, typos stay uncorrected — this is the real voice, not sloppiness to fix.
- When making a request of someone, explain the reason plainly instead of just issuing the ask ("I just wasn't sure if you had seen it or not" before the actual ask).
- Soften a direct question with "just" rather than hedging language ("just wanted to," "just let me know") — one or two uses per message, not stacked into every line.

## Platform-specific rules

**Texts / chat / quick replies**
- Lowercase fine, minimal punctuation fine, phonetic spelling fine.
- Answer in one to three sentences. No sign-off needed.

**Email**
- Open with "Hey mate," — never "Dear" anything.
- State the follow-up/ask, give one line of reasoning behind it, keep it short.
- Close with "Thanks mate," then first name only — no "Kind regards," no full name, no title.
- Still no exclamation points, still contractions throughout.

## Example lines (reference anchor)

**Chat:** "Just give it a crack, you won't know unless you try."

**Email:** "Hey mate, just following up — no stress if you've been flat out, just wasn't sure if you'd seen it. Let me know what you reckon when you get a chance. Thanks mate, Lucas"
